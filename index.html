The vision

What it is, in two halves

The games-education field is mid-realignment. In eighteen months the Cxmmunity HBCU league went dark, HSEL was absorbed into PlayVS, Microsoft's TEALS wound down, and Twitch's student scholarship has been dormant since 2021. The companies behind those programs did not stop caring. They lost the vehicle. There is real money in this field looking for a home, and nobody is building the home.

Half one · the Directory

Every credible path into game-making, honestly cataloged. It answers the donor question: where do dollars go furthest?

Every entry carries evidence and a verification date. Badges read VERIFIED, SOURCED, or VERIFY, which means we publish our own gaps. Nobody else in this space does that, and it is why a board can trust it.

Half two · the Pipeline

Opt-in profiles of scholars and alumni that recruiters can hire from. Different question: who is ready?

Consent first and student controlled. Recruiters request an introduction and GGP makes it. No browsing, no cold outreach, no contact details without the student's yes.

"I wish we could've gotten to know other CYSTEM students at other schools." KB, USC · Year 1 exit survey

That was the loudest finding at every school. The Directory is that complaint answered and made durable, then extended out to alumni and industry. Your alumni list is the seed. Your Discord is where it lives day to day. Your mentor call fills the mentor column. It is not a fourth thing to maintain. It is a spine for the three you already maintain.

The one risk worth holding the line on

Pipeline consent. If a scholar's information ever reaches a recruiter without that scholar's explicit yes, the trust that makes this work is gone and does not come back. The standing rule: any consent failure pauses the Pipeline entirely, whatever it costs the calendar. Better to ship late than ship that wrong. You are the owner now, so that line is yours to hold too.

Only you can answer these

Four questions, and then this moves

Everything else on this page has an owner who is not you. These four do not move without you. Type as much or as little as you want, it saves as you go, and there is a copy button at the bottom.

1. Does the Directory launch to your scholars first, or to the field first?

Scholars first means your cohort gets a head start on September internship applications and you find the bugs with a friendly audience. Field first means a bigger splash but you are debugging in public.

2. Does the alumni list seed the Pipeline, and on what terms?

Your 18 alumni with where they landed is the strongest asset anyone has produced for this. It could seed the Pipeline immediately. It is also their data, so nobody touches it without a rule you set.

3. Who is the data steward?

One person accountable for verification and corrections after launch. Roughly a few hours a month once the quarterly refresh is automated. An Extended Scholar doing this gets a real, nameable credential out of it.

4. Is October 9 real?

It sits ahead of a funder's fall letter-of-intent deadline on purpose, so there is a reason for it. But you know what your fall actually looks like, and a date nobody can hit costs more than a date that moves now.
